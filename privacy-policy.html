Orbit pop - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Orbit pop ("the app", "the game") is a free arcade game published by Joy Space ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The app saves a few things only on your device: your top scores for each game mode. This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain information to show ads and measure their performance, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device and app information (model, operating system, app version), and how you interact with ads. We do not receive your personal identity from this process.

In regions where the law requires it (such as the EEA and the UK), the app shows a consent form from Google so you can choose how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice from the app when the form is available.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. How we use information

Information handled by the app is used only to run the game, save your scores, and show ads. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Scores saved on your device are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Data held by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies linked above.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google. You can also contact us with any question.

7. Security

We keep the app simple and do not store personal data on our own servers. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date updated above.
